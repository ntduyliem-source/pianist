PIANIST METHOD BOOK SOURCES — acquired 2026-10-25

The catalog describes available exercises, not complete books unless explicitly
stated. Original exercise numbers and both structural staves are preserved.
Original source MXL files are byte-identical. WhiteNote's raw XML files are
wrapped in MXL archives without changing the XML bytes. No fingering, pitches,
rhythms, repeats, dynamics, fonts or source rights notices were invented/rewritten.

These individual scores are recorded as CC0 with license_conflict=False in the
PDMX metadata. Per-score MuseScore links and exact source hashes are in catalog.json.
The original MXL bytes from the existing local source warehouse are retained;
the ABC round trips and generated exercise banks were not used.

The app can display fingerings when the source contains them. Ordinary MIDI
reports keys and timing, not which finger played a key; it cannot grade fingering.
